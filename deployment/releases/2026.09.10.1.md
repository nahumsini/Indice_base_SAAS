## Schema and subsequent releases

The public schema advances from V265 to V269. Only the budget rollout and occurrence tables
are added; historical finance rows and applied Flyway checksums are preserved.
Generation is first disabled, then enabled after preservation checks. Monthly obligations
are idempotent, retain their relationship to the budget and do not create payments.
Ambiguous historical matches require review instead of automatic historical debt creation.

**V266–V268 from main are not in this artifact.** A later release of that broader line must
explicitly review and rehearse application of those pending migrations on an isolated copy
of V269, including startup, regressions and rollback. Normal Flyway startup must not be
assumed to execute lower versions. Never repair or edit history to hide this difference,
and never replace an already applied migration.
