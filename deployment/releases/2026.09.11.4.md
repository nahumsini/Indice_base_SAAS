Product images in the public Sales catalog use private MinIO objects with signed browser URLs. Those URLs expire after 15 minutes, while the catalog previously loaded its bootstrap only once. A product cover already in browser cache could remain visible while an unvisited gallery image failed, and the gallery then displayed the empty-image fallback even though the object existed.

The public catalog now coalesces bootstrap refreshes, silently renews media links every 10 minutes, renews them when an online tab becomes visible again, and retries the selected gallery image once with a fresh URL after a load error. The open gallery follows refreshed product data, and image downloads obtain fresh links immediately before downloading. Catalog identity, public-token validation, object privacy, cart state and purchase-request behavior remain unchanged.
